at commit 4068240525a42e55542277af2ebf0c617c82e38b, notably source/accepted revision,
resolve-policy-invoke-classify, bounded approval and observable readiness semantics.
Source: https://github.com/w8123/EnterpriseAgentFramework/tree/4068240525a42e55542277af2ebf0c617c82e38b

or that Agents JS, DBOS or assistant-ui have passed qualification.

are retained under [release operations evidence](qualification/release-operations/).
