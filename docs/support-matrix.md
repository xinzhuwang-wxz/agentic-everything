# Declared support and operational ownership

This matrix is scoped to the repository's recorded evidence, not a promise of arbitrary-host or production support. Runtime/adapter rows have independent evidence; an operational pass does not silently promote an untested adapter. [Current full acceptance status](acceptance-status.md) remains authoritative for the assembled product.
